# SALMON PDF Visual Identity Notes

## Colour Palette (from PDF)
- **Primary dark red/crimson**: #8B1A2B (deep burgundy/maroon — used for header backgrounds, section title pills, accent bars)
- **Secondary red**: #C0392B (brighter red — used for decorative elements, borders)
- **Warm cream/parchment**: #F5F0E0 (page background, card backgrounds)
- **Dark navy/charcoal**: #1A1A2E or #2C2C3E (body text)
- **Gold/tan accent**: #C8A96E (border accents, divider lines)
- **White**: #FFFFFF (text on dark backgrounds)

## Typography
- **Headings**: Bold sans-serif (appears to be a condensed bold — like Oswald or similar)
- **Body**: Regular weight sans-serif
- **Section pills/labels**: White bold text on crimson rounded-rectangle backgrounds

## Design Motifs
- **Northwest Coast formline border pattern**: repeating geometric grid pattern on left/right margins (dark red on cream)
- **Salmon formline artwork**: large circular medallion with Indigenous salmon artwork (black, red, cream)
- **Rounded rectangle cards**: content sits in cream-coloured rounded-corner boxes with subtle gold/tan borders
- **Crimson section divider bars**: thin horizontal bars between sections
- **Salmon icon**: small salmon icon in bottom-right corner of pages

## Key Colours to Apply to Site
- Replace copper (#B87333) with the PDF's crimson/burgundy: #8B1A2B
- Keep warm cream background: matches PDF parchment
- Add formline geometric border motif as a subtle accent
- Section title pills: white text on crimson rounded rectangles
