# SALMON Tool — Design Brainstorm

## Context
The SALMON Tool is an Indigenous-informed teacher evaluation framework developed in collaboration with the Stó:lō Education Committee at Seabird Island Community School. SALMON = Seabird Assessment for Learning, Mentorship & Ongoing Nurturing. The site needs to honor Indigenous values, relational accountability, and the natural metaphor of the salmon lifecycle.

## Idea A — "River Stone & Cedar" (Pacific Northwest Organic)

**Design Movement:** Pacific Northwest Organic — inspired by the textures of river stones, cedar bark, and the deep greens and ochres of the Fraser Valley.

**Core Principles:**
1. Organic asymmetry — layouts that breathe like a forest, never rigid grids
2. Tactile texture — paper grain, stone-like surfaces, woven patterns as dividers
3. Warm restraint — rich earth tones used sparingly against cream/linen backgrounds
4. Narrative flow — the page reads like a river, guiding the eye downstream

**Color Philosophy:** Deep cedar brown (#3D2B1F), river-stone slate (#5C6B73), warm ochre/amber (#C97B2E), and a soft linen cream (#F5F0E8). The palette evokes the natural landscape of the Fraser River Valley without being cliché. Ochre is the accent — used for CTAs and highlights, echoing the salmon's orange roe.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial — hero text bleeds left, imagery anchors right. Sections alternate between full-bleed image strips and narrow text columns. A vertical timeline for the four process stages runs down the left margin.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Woven cedar-pattern SVG dividers between sections
2. Circular image crops echoing the circular nature of salmon lifecycle
3. Handwritten-style accent text for section labels

**Interaction Philosophy:** Slow, deliberate scroll reveals — content fades in from below like sediment settling. Hover states feel tactile, with subtle texture shifts.

**Animation:** Fade-up on scroll with 0.4s ease-out. Hover on cards lifts with a soft shadow. Stage tabs animate with a sliding underline.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (serif, italic for emphasis)
- Body: Source Serif 4 (readable, warm)
- Labels: Libre Baskerville Small Caps

## Idea B — "Deep Water & Copper" (Indigenous Contemporary)

**Design Movement:** Indigenous Contemporary — clean modernism filtered through Pacific Northwest Coast art motifs. Think of a museum exhibition for living culture.

**Core Principles:**
1. Structural boldness — strong typographic hierarchy, wide margins, deliberate negative space
2. Cultural motif integration — subtle formline-inspired geometric borders and dividers
3. Deep contrast — near-black backgrounds with copper and cream accents
4. Cyclical narrative — visual language that reinforces the salmon's return journey

**Color Philosophy:** Deep river navy (#1A2535), copper/salmon (#C4622D), warm cream (#F2EAD8), and sage green (#6B8C6B). The dark background evokes deep water — where salmon journey unseen. Copper is the accent, referencing both the salmon's flesh and Pacific Northwest metalwork traditions.

**Layout Paradigm:** Magazine-style with a strong left-rail navigation. The homepage is a single long scroll with anchored sections. Each stage card uses a bold number + large type treatment.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin copper horizontal rules as section dividers
2. Bold stage numbers in a large serif, partially cropped at the edge
3. A salmon-lifecycle progress indicator in the sticky header

**Interaction Philosophy:** Confident and direct — interactions are purposeful. Hover reveals additional context. The lifecycle stages are navigable via a sticky tab bar.

**Animation:** Section transitions use a horizontal wipe. Stage cards expand on click with a smooth height animation. The hero has a slow-pan parallax on the background image.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (high-contrast serif, elegant)
- Body: Lora (warm, readable serif)
- UI/Labels: DM Sans (clean, modern contrast)

## Idea C — "Watershed" (Flowing Editorial)

**Design Movement:** Flowing Editorial — inspired by the movement of water, combining the clarity of editorial design with organic, fluid shapes.

**Core Principles:**
1. Flow over structure — curved section dividers, wave-like transitions between content areas
2. Light and depth — layered translucent panels creating a sense of depth like looking through water
3. Generous whitespace — content breathes, never crowded
4. Warmth through texture — subtle noise/grain overlay on backgrounds

**Color Philosophy:** Forest green (#2D5016), warm amber (#D4831A), off-white (#FAF7F2), and muted teal (#3D7A6E). Green grounds the site in the land; amber echoes the salmon's journey and the warmth of community; off-white provides breathing room.

**Layout Paradigm:** Flowing single-page with wave-shaped SVG section breaks. The four lifecycle stages are presented as a horizontal scrolling carousel on desktop, stacked vertically on mobile. Process steps use a diagonal timeline.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Wave/water SVG dividers between major sections
2. Leaf/water-drop shaped image masks
3. A subtle animated water ripple on the hero

**Interaction Philosophy:** Gentle and inviting — the site feels like a guided walk along a riverbank. Interactions are smooth and unhurried.

**Animation:** Wave dividers animate subtly on scroll. Cards have a gentle float animation. Text reveals use a staggered fade-in.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Fraunces (optical-size serif, expressive)
- Body: Nunito (friendly, rounded, accessible)
- Accent: Playfair Display Italic for pull quotes

## Selected Approach: **Idea B — "Deep Water & Copper"**

This approach best honors the gravitas and cultural significance of the SALMON framework. The dark, deep-water aesthetic gives the site a museum-quality presence appropriate for showcasing Indigenous educational research. The copper accent color directly references both the salmon's flesh and Pacific Northwest metalwork traditions. The strong typographic hierarchy ensures the rich content is readable and navigable.
